Jeju-born Korean Independence Activist · 1924–present

강태선 姜太善

Kang Tae-sun

“일본은 반드시 패망할 것” “Japan will surely be defeated.”

A clandestine nationalist in wartime Osaka. The strongest case of how an ordinary colonized Korean turned fear and rumor into political hope — and Jeju's only living officially recognized independence patriot.
